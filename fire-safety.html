Our Product Range

FIRE SAFETY PRODUCTS

From addressable fire alarm systems to complete turnkey fire suppression installations — Formach supplies all the equipment needed to protect industrial facilities, warehouses, hotels and public buildings.

🔴

Fire Alarm Control Panels

Conventional and addressable fire alarm control panels. 2 to 32 loop systems for small and large installations. EN 54 certified.

💨

Smoke & Heat Detectors

Optical smoke detectors, ionisation detectors, heat detectors and multi-sensor detectors. Addressable and conventional types.

💧

Sprinkler Systems

Wet, dry and pre-action sprinkler systems. Concealed and upright sprinkler heads. NFPA and EN 12845 compliant components.

🧯

Fire Extinguishers

Portable and wheeled fire extinguishers — water, foam, CO2, dry powder and clean agent types. All sizes and classes.

💡

Emergency & Exit Lighting

Emergency luminaires, exit sign lights and maintained/non-maintained emergency lighting systems for safe evacuation.

🔵

Gas Detection Systems

Fixed and portable gas detectors for CO, H2S, LPG, LNG, O2 and toxic gases. For industrial and confined space applications.

📢

Voice Alarm Systems

Public address and voice alarm systems for fire evacuation in large buildings, hotels and public facilities.

🔧

Fire-Rated Cables & Accessories

Fire-resistant and fire-rated cables, fireproof junction boxes and all accessories for fire alarm and suppression wiring.
