Market Context

AFRICA'S INFRASTRUCTURE OPPORTUNITY

Francophone Africa is experiencing significant infrastructure investment, driven by government programs, development finance institutions (AfDB, World Bank, AFD, KfW) and the private sector. Road construction, water supply, mining and urban development projects are creating sustained demand for industrial machinery and equipment across the region.

For contractors and operators working in these markets, sourcing the right equipment at the right price is a competitive advantage. Turkey has emerged as an ideal sourcing base — offering European-quality machinery at prices significantly below European suppliers, with shorter lead times than China and better spare parts availability.

Key Countries and Their Equipment Needs

Mali

Mali's construction sector is driven by road construction programs, urban development and mining. Key equipment needs include crushing and screening plants for road aggregate, asphalt mixing plants, concrete batching plants, water supply infrastructure (pumps, valves, pipes) and general construction equipment. Formach Group has active client relationships in Mali including ECGF SA.

Sénégal

Senegal's Plan Sénégal Emergent has driven significant infrastructure investment. Active equipment needs include water treatment equipment, industrial pumps, electrical cables and components, fire safety systems and general industrial supplies. Formach Group supplies clients in Senegal including CFTS and BECM-CG.

Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire is one of the fastest-growing economies in Africa. Major infrastructure projects in Abidjan and across the country create strong demand for BTP equipment, electrical infrastructure and industrial machinery. The country's port makes it an attractive entry point for equipment destined for the broader UEMOA zone.

Cameroun

Cameroun's mining sector and road construction programs create sustained demand for crushing equipment, asphalt plants and general industrial supplies. Formach Group has an established client relationship with Société Maina Cameroun.

Guinea, Burkina Faso, Niger, Congo

These markets are driven primarily by mining projects (gold, bauxite, iron ore, uranium) creating demand for heavy equipment, industrial pumps, electrical infrastructure and maintenance supplies.

Practical Considerations for African Buyers

Customs Documentation: Ensure all equipment comes with complete customs documentation — commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and CE declaration. Incomplete documentation is the most common cause of delays and additional costs at African ports.

Voltage and Frequency: Confirm the local power supply standard (most francophone Africa uses 220V/50Hz) and ensure equipment is configured accordingly before shipment.

Spare Parts Planning: Order critical spare parts with the initial equipment shipment. Lead times for spare parts from Turkey are typically 2-4 weeks, which can be costly if a machine is idle waiting for a part.

Working with Formach Group: As a Turkey-based intermediary fluent in French and English, Formach Group removes the complexity of sourcing from Turkey. We handle supplier identification, technical verification, negotiation, quality control, export documentation and freight forwarding — delivering to your port or site in Africa.
